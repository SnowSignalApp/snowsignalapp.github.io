Privacy policy

Effective September 16, 2026

SnowSignal is designed to work without a customer account. This policy describes the current app and website while they are in development and will be updated if the data practices change before release.

Information SnowSignal handles

  • Favorite resorts, units, language, forecast preferences, and Premium state are stored on your device.
  • When the app contacts the SnowSignal service, hosting infrastructure may process standard request information such as IP address, request time, route, device network information, and diagnostic logs for security and reliability.
  • SnowSignal sends resort coordinates and requested forecast settings—not your identity—to weather providers through its backend.
  • The current consumer app does not request precise device location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or payment-card details.

Accounts and purchases

The consumer app does not currently require an account. Sign in with Apple is limited to authorized owner administration. If subscriptions launch, Apple will process purchases; SnowSignal will not receive your full payment-card details.

Providers and hosting

SnowSignal uses service providers for hosting, forecasts, resort data, imagery, and optional live operating status. These providers process only the data necessary to deliver their service and are subject to their own policies. Provider requests are cached to reduce data transfer and unnecessary repeated processing.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current app does not include a third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDK. SnowSignal does not sell personal information. Any future analytics or advertising change will require a policy and App Privacy update before release.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes locally stored customer preferences and favorites. Operational server logs are retained only as needed for security, debugging, and reliable operation according to the hosting configuration. Contact support with a privacy question or deletion request relating to server-side information.

Children and safety

SnowSignal is not directed to children under 13. Forecasts and resort data are informational and are not a substitute for official avalanche, closure, patrol, or emergency guidance.
