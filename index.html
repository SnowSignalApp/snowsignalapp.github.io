Data principles

Useful, current, and attributed.

Forecasts are cached to reduce provider load and carry their update time. Missing, stale, unsupported, or uncertain information is hidden or clearly labeled—never invented.

Mountain weatherOpen-Meteo for elevation-aware forecasts and worldwide ranking weather.
Resorts and pistesOpenSkiData, OpenStreetMap, OpenSkiMap, Skimap.org, Who's On First, and Mapterhorn, with curated official-source corrections.
Maps and imageryApple Maps for realistic-elevation terrain; licensed resort imagery and attributed Wikimedia Commons photography where available.
Live resort feedsCurrent eligible reports from open·piste, Fnugg, and Lumiplan. Liftie support remains pending provider approval and is not required for launch.
WebcamsWindy Webcams, shown only where the production API and nearby camera coverage are available.
