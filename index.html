Built for the question skiers ask

Honest resort data

Skiable lifts, slopes, and kilometers stay separate from hiking, Nordic, touring, sledding, and ordinary transport data.

Data principles

Useful, current, and attributed.

Forecasts are cached to reduce provider load and clearly carry their update time. Missing or stale information is never invented. Weather data is attributed to Open-Meteo; resort catalog data includes OpenStreetMap and OpenSkiData attribution in the app.
